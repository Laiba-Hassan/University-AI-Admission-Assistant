# Fee Payment, Instalments and Refund Policy

**How fees are structured.** Tuition is charged per semester for most programs, and per year for the MBA. Some items are one-time charges (admission fee, security deposit). Fees differ by campus and by whether the student is local or international, so always tell us your campus and student type when asking about a fee.

**Due dates.** Semester fees fall due before the semester starts. A late fee applies after the grace period. Students with unpaid dues cannot sit examinations.

**Instalments.** Students may apply to split a semester's tuition into two instalments. Apply through the student portal before the due date. Instalment approval is at the discretion of the accounts office.

**Refunds.** If you withdraw before classes start, tuition already paid is refunded except for the admission fee and application processing fee. If you withdraw within the first two weeks of the semester, a partial tuition refund is given. After that no tuition refund is given. Hostel fees are refunded on a pro-rata basis. The refundable security deposit is returned after hostel clearance.

**Fee changes.** Fees are set per academic year and may change for later years. The published figure is the one that applies to the academic year shown next to it.
