# Contact, Campus Locations and Visiting (Demo — fictional data)

**Lahore Main Campus** (fictional address): Crescent Avenue, Lahore. **Islamabad Campus** (fictional address): Institutional Area, Islamabad.

**Admissions office hours:** Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm; Saturday 9am to 1pm; closed Sunday and on public holidays.

**Ways to reach us:** the contact form on the website, this chat assistant (24 hours, with staff following up during office hours), or a phone call during office hours. Sensitive matters such as individual fee concessions, exceptions to deadlines and document verification are handled by staff, not by this assistant.

**Visiting.** Campus tours run weekdays by appointment. Bring your CNIC. Visitors to the hostel must be accompanied by admissions staff.
