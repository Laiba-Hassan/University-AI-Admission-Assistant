# Faculty of Computing — Programs Overview

**BS Computer Science (BSCS).** Our flagship program: programming, data structures, algorithms, operating systems, databases, networks and a final-year project. Offered at both campuses.

**BS Software Engineering (BSSE).** Focuses on how large software is designed, tested and maintained, with team projects and industry-style processes.

**BS Artificial Intelligence (BSAI).** Machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing and computer vision on top of a strong mathematics core.

**BS Data Science (BSDS).** Statistics, data engineering, analytics and machine learning. Offered at the Islamabad campus.

**MS Computer Science (MSCS).** A two-year research-oriented master's with coursework and a thesis or project option.

Graduates work as software engineers, data scientists, AI engineers and researchers. The Faculty runs an annual project showcase where students present final-year work to industry visitors.
