# About Crescent Valley University (Demo — fictional data)

Crescent Valley University is a fictional private university created for product demonstration. It has three faculties: Computing, Business & Management, and Engineering. It operates two campuses, Lahore Main Campus and Islamabad Campus.

The university's motto is "Learn. Build. Lead." Teaching combines classroom instruction with lab work, projects and internships. Class sizes are kept small in first-year courses so that new students get individual attention.

Academic year: Fall semester starts in September and Spring semester starts in February. Each semester is about 16 weeks including examinations.
