# Academic Rules

**Grading.** Courses are graded on a 4.0 scale. CGPA is the average across all completed courses weighted by credit hours.

**Attendance.** A minimum attendance is required in every course to be eligible for the final examination. Students below the minimum are barred from that course's final exam and must repeat it.

**Academic probation.** A student whose CGPA falls below 2.0 is placed on probation for one semester with a reduced course load and mandatory advising. Failure to recover leads to a review by the academic committee.

**Repeating courses.** Students may repeat a course to improve a low grade; the new grade replaces the old one in the CGPA, and both appear on the transcript.

**Plagiarism and cheating.** These are treated as serious offences. The first confirmed offence usually means zero in the assessment; repeated offences may lead to suspension.

**Freezing a semester.** A student may freeze up to two semesters during the degree, with prior approval, for medical or personal reasons.
