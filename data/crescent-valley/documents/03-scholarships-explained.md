# Scholarships and Financial Aid Explained

Crescent Valley offers six scholarship and aid programs. Full criteria, coverage and conditions are held in the structured scholarship records; this page explains how they fit together.

**Crescent Valley Merit Scholarship.** For students who do very well in both the CET and intermediate. It is renewed every semester, so keeping your CGPA up matters. If your CGPA drops below the renewal threshold you lose it for the following semester and can regain it by meeting the threshold again.

**Board Topper Award.** Recognises top board positions. It is the most generous award and is given once per student. You need to bring your board certificate.

**Sibling Discount.** Applies to tuition when more than one sibling is enrolled at the same time. It stops when a sibling graduates or leaves.

**Need-Based Financial Grant.** For families who cannot meet the full fee. Applicants submit income documents and attend an interview with the Financial Aid Committee. The committee decides the percentage case by case. The grant is reviewed each year.

**Women in Engineering Grant.** Encourages female applicants to engineering programs.

**Hafiz-e-Quran Concession.** For certified huffaz.

**Combining awards.** In general only one merit-type award is allowed per student. The Sibling Discount is the main one that can be added to another award. Where two awards cannot be combined, the student receives the one with the higher value. Ask admissions if your situation is unclear.

Scholarship percentages apply to tuition only, never to hostel or admission fees.
