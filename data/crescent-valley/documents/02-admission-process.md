# The Admission Process

1. **Choose programs.** You may list up to three program preferences in priority order.
2. **Apply online.** Create an account on the admissions portal, complete the application form and upload scanned documents.
3. **Pay the application processing fee.** The application is not reviewed until this fee is paid.
4. **Take the Crescent Entry Test (CET).** Book a test slot in the portal. The CET covers mathematics, English and analytical reasoning. Business applicants take a business section in place of some mathematics content.
5. **Merit list.** Applicants are ranked by a weighted score of the CET and intermediate marks. Merit lists are published on the portal.
6. **Offer and confirmation.** Selected students receive an offer letter with a fee-payment deadline. A seat is confirmed once the admission fee and first-semester dues are paid.
7. **Orientation.** New students attend a one-week orientation before classes begin.

Exact dates and fees for each intake are published separately and should always be checked with the admissions office or the structured fee and intake information.
