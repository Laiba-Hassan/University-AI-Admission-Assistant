# Hostel and Campus Life

Both campuses have on-campus hostels for male and female students in separate blocks. Rooms are shared (two or three students) and include beds, study desks, wardrobes and Wi-Fi. Meals are served in the campus cafeteria and the hostel mess.

Hostel rules: visitors are allowed in the common area only; curfew is 10pm for female blocks and 11pm for male blocks; smoking is prohibited. A hostel warden is on site at all times.

Rooms are allocated after fee payment and are limited, so apply early. Students living within the city are usually asked to commute unless they have a documented reason.

Campus life includes societies (computing, debate, sports, drama, entrepreneurship), an annual sports week and a technology festival each spring.
