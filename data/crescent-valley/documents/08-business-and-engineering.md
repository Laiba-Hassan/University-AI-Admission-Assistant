# Business & Management and Engineering — Programs Overview

## Faculty of Business & Management
**BBA** covers management, marketing, finance and entrepreneurship, with a final-semester capstone. **BS Accounting & Finance** prepares students for ACCA and CFA routes and includes financial reporting and auditing. **BS Economics** (Islamabad) combines micro and macroeconomics, econometrics and public policy. **MBA** is a two-year evening program for working professionals with electives in finance, marketing and operations.

## Faculty of Engineering
**BS Electrical Engineering** (power, electronics, control, communications), **BS Mechanical Engineering** (thermodynamics, design, manufacturing), **BS Civil Engineering** (structures, transportation, geotechnical; Islamabad campus) and **BS Chemical Engineering** (process design, reaction engineering). Engineering students complete a final-year design project and a summer internship. Graduates seek registration with the Pakistan Engineering Council.
