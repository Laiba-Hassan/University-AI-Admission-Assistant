# Guide for International Students

International applicants follow the same application steps as local students, with these differences:

- Fees are charged in US dollars under the international fee structure, which is separate from the local structure.
- Submit a passport copy, your academic transcripts and, for foreign qualifications, an equivalence certificate from the Inter Board Committee of Chairmen (IBCC).
- The CET can be taken online from your home country. Ask admissions for the remote-test procedure.
- After you accept your offer, the university issues a letter that supports your student visa application. You are responsible for the visa itself.
- International students may live in the hostel subject to availability.

Overseas Pakistanis with a valid NICOP may apply under the local fee structure. Contact admissions with your NICOP to confirm eligibility.
