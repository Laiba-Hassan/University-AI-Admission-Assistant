# About Nexora University (Demo — fictional data)

Nexora University is a fictional, small, technology and social-sciences focused university in Karachi with two schools: the School of Technology and the School of Social Sciences & Media. Its motto is "Begin something new." Nexora is known for small classes, project-based learning and a modern approach to pricing: students pay per credit hour, so they can control their semester cost by choosing their course load.
