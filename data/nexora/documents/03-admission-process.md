# Nexora Admission Process

Apply online, pay the admission fee, sit the Nexora Aptitude Test (NAT) and, for psychology and media programs, attend an interview or submit a portfolio. Offers are sent by email within two weeks of the test. Accept your offer by paying the first-semester dues before the date in the letter. Orientation takes place in the week before classes.
