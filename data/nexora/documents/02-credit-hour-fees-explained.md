# How Per-Credit-Hour Fees Work

Every course carries a number of credit hours, usually 3. Tuition for a semester is the program's per-credit-hour rate times the credit hours you register. Registering for fewer credit hours lowers the semester total, but your degree will take longer to finish. Fixed items such as the one-time admission fee and the semester student-services fee are charged in addition to tuition. Scholarships reduce the per-credit-hour tuition, not the fixed items. A semester total should always be calculated from the current rate; do not estimate it.
