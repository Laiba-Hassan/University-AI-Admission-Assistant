# Privacy Policy — STARTING DRAFT (not legal advice; lawyer review required before use)

**Platform:** [PLATFORM NAME] ("we"). We provide an AI admissions assistant to universities ("Universities"), which appears on their websites and on WhatsApp.

## Who is responsible for your data
For students who chat with a University's assistant, the **University is the controller** and we act as its **processor**. For University staff accounts and platform sign-up requests, we are the controller.

## What we collect
- Messages you send (text; and the transcript of voice notes/recordings). **Audio is deleted right after transcription and is not stored.**
- Details you choose to give: name, phone/email, program interest, and your consent.
- Technical data: channel, language, timestamps, anonymous session id or WhatsApp ID.
- Staff data: email, role, notification preferences.

## How we use it
To answer your questions from the University's approved information, to pass your request to University staff when you ask or when we cannot answer, and to keep the service safe and working. Phone numbers and names are masked in stored messages and never appear in staff alerts.

## Who we share it with (sub-processors — see `subprocessors.md`)
Model provider (Google Gemini), hosting/database, Meta (WhatsApp), email provider, error monitoring. We do not sell personal data.

## Retention and your rights
Each University sets a retention period (default 180 days), after which conversations are deleted. You can ask the University (or us, for staff data) to export or delete your data; we support export and delete requests.

## Security
Each University's data is isolated at the database level; access is logged; data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
