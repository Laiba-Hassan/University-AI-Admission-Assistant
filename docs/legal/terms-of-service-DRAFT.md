# Terms of Service — DRAFT (not legal advice; lawyer review required before this is treated as final)

These terms govern a University's use of Enrollium (the platform providing an AI admissions assistant embedded
on the University's website and, where connected, WhatsApp) and a University staff member's use of its
dashboard.

## The service

Enrollium answers prospective-student questions from a University's own approved knowledge base (programs, fees,
intakes, requirements, scholarships, FAQs), captures leads with the student's consent, and hands a conversation
to University staff when asked or when it cannot answer. Answers are checked against the University's own stored
facts before being shown; the platform does not invent figures.

## Accounts and access

A University's account starts as a signup request, reviewed and approved by a platform administrator before any
tenant is created. Staff accounts within an approved University are invited by that University's own admins and
carry one of three roles (Admin, Editor, Viewer), each with a different, enforced level of access — an
enforcement kept honest by this platform's own automated tests, not just documentation.

## Acceptable use

A University may not use the platform to publish false or misleading information to prospective students, to
collect personal data without the consent this platform's own lead-capture flow requires, or to attempt to
bypass the tenant isolation, rate limiting, or verification-before-answering safeguards built into the platform.

## Plans, limits and billing

A University's plan sets its monthly conversation and message caps, visible in its own Settings → Usage. **In-app
billing (a real payment gateway) is not yet built** — billing today is tracked manually by the platform operator
(see the Super Admin "Billing" page's own "invoiced outside platform" field) until a real payment integration
ships. No payment card is charged by this platform automatically.

## Data ownership and export

A University owns the data it and its applicants generate. It can export that data at any time (Settings →
Retention → "Export data"). See the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) and [Data Processing Agreement
template](../../docs/legal/dpa-template-DRAFT.md) for how that data is handled and for how long it's retained by
default.
